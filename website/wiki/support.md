# Support Learnarr

Learnarr is free, has no ads, no paid tier and no tracking, and it will stay that way. It's built in spare
time by two people who wanted to learn languages from their own media.

<SupportLinks />

## Other ways to help

- **Tell people about it.** A post on r/selfhosted, r/languagelearning or your favourite forum helps more than
  you'd think.
- **Report what's broken.** A clear [issue](https://github.com/smoothless-io/learnarr/issues) with the steps to
  reproduce it is a real contribution.
- **Translate the interface** into your language: see [Translating](./translating).
- **Check a language.** If you speak a language Learnarr lemmatises, open an episode in it and tell us where
  the dictionary forms or "% you know" look wrong.
- **Write code or docs.** See [Contributing](./contributing). This wiki has an _Edit this page_ link at the
  bottom of every page.
- **Star the [repository](https://github.com/smoothless-io/learnarr)** on GitHub, so others find it.
